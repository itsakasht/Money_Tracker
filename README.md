# Money Tracker

A lightweight, privacy-focused, money tracking application you can use to track your expenses and incomes. It syncs directly with your Google Drive so you can view it on multiple mobile and desktop devices. I made this over a weekend and intend to keep it this super simple and easy to use.

---

## Getting Started

Money Tracker runs directly in any modern web browser or as a standalone app installed on your mobile phone. It will work on both android and iOS. It maintains local offline capabilities with seamless cloud synchronization.

### Installing the App

You do not need to download an APK or configure developer environments to use the app, simply:

1. Open the hosted [Money Tracker App](https://itsakasht.github.io/Money_Tracker/MoneyTrackerApp.html) URL in **Chrome on Android** or **Safari on iOS** or **On any browser on Desktop**.
2. Open your mobile browser menu (the three-dot menu in Chrome or the Share icon in Safari).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**.
4. On desktop browser go to **Settings** > **Save and Share** > **Install page as app**
5. The app will launch full-screen directly from your home screen/start menu and remain fully functional offline.

### Using of the App

1. **Logging Entries:** Select either Expense or Income, input the amount, pick the correct category, set the date, add an optional note/description, and click save.
2. **Splitting Bills:** Enable the bill splitting toggle on expense entries to split costs dynamically among multiple friends.
3. **Tracking Debts:** Use the People Ledger search bar to look up specific names and view outstanding balances or past transactions.
4. **Visualize spending on Calendar:** Review spending habits via category breakdown charts and inspect daily activity through an interactive calendar grid.
5. **Filtering by Date:** Tap any date block in the Activity Grid calendar to filter visible logs for that specific day.
6. **Category Breakdown:** View monthly or all time category wise expenses. You can also add custom categories if needed.

### Setting Up Google Drive

1. Open the Money Tracker app on your device.
2. Scroll to the **Google Drive Cloud Sync** card located at the bottom of the page.
3. Tap **Connect Drive**.
4. Sign in with your Google account and approve the permission prompt.
5. The app automatically manages a private `money_tracker.csv` file in your Google Drive cloud, ensuring your financial logs stay synchronized across all your devices.
